3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website uses a limited number of cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your computer, mobile device, or browser that allow websites and service providers to recognize a browser or remember certain information.

We may use the following categories of cookies:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support basic Website operation, security, navigation, and form functionality. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the Website from malicious traffic and automated abuse. Disabling these cookies may prevent portions of the Website from functioning correctly.

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the Website to remember selections or provide enhanced functionality.

Website Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors use the Website. Cloudflare Web Analytics collects information such as the pages viewed, the referring website, browser and device type, approximate location derived from an IP address, and page-performance measurements, and reports it to us in aggregate.

According to Cloudflare, Web Analytics does not use cookies or similar client-side storage to collect these measurements and does not track individuals across websites. We use analytics information to evaluate Website performance and improve the content and user experience, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Spam and Abuse Protection

Our contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish people from automated bots. When the contact form loads and when you submit it, Turnstile collects technical information from your browser and device, such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, and signals about how the page was loaded and used, and Cloudflare uses that information to determine whether the request is likely automated. Cloudflare processes this information under its own privacy policy.

Turnstile is used only to protect the Website and our team from spam and abuse. Recon receives the result of the check, not a profile of you.

Third-Party and Embedded-Content Cookies

The Website may contain embedded YouTube videos, Google Maps, or other third-party content. When you interact with embedded content, the third-party provider may collect information from your browser or device and may place its own cookies or similar technologies.

The information collected by a third-party service is governed by that provider’s privacy policy and settings, rather than this Privacy Policy.

Managing Cookies

The Website does not use advertising cookies, and our analytics do not rely on cookies, so the Website does not display a cookie-consent banner. You may configure your browser to block, restrict, or delete cookies.

Blocking cookies may affect the availability or operation of certain Website features. Your browser or device settings may also allow you to control cookies and similar technologies used by third-party providers.
